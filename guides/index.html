Guides

Each guide works one hedge through the calculator with illustrative prices, explains the numbers and opens the same scenario in the calculator to change.

  • A price floor for soybeans with puts. How a soybean producer sets a minimum sale price with put options on CBOT futures: what the put costs, the floor in cents a bushel and dollars a ton, and how many contracts 5,000 tons need.
  • A zero-cost collar on coffee. How a coffee exporter buys a put and sells a call that pays for it: the call’s strike found with the calculator, and the floor and the ceiling in cents a pound and dollars a 60-kg bag.
  • A cost cap for a crude oil buyer with calls. How a buyer of crude oil caps its purchase price with call options on WTI futures: what the call costs, the cap in dollars a barrel, and what happens below the strike.
  • A price floor for cattle with live cattle puts. How a cattle producer sets a minimum price with put options on CME live cattle futures: the put’s cost, the floor in cents a pound and dollars a kilo, and how many contracts 100 head need.
  • How many contracts for 5,000 tons. How many futures or options contracts hedge 5,000 metric tons of soybeans, wheat, corn, sugar, cotton or Rosario’s grains, and what the nearest whole numbers leave short or over.

The prices in these guides are illustrative, not market data, and their figures are the calculator’s for them. Nothing here is investment advice.
