Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

Thank you for visiting Commodity Options Calculator. This website provides an educational tool for simulating options pricing for commodity futures: metals, energy, grains, softs and livestock.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information from users. The calculator runs in your browser. The one part of this site that runs on a server is the market data function described in section 3. It receives only the name of the commodity you asked about, along with what every web request carries, which section 3 explains.

This site does not show ads yet. We plan to show them through Google AdSense; section 2 says what that will mean, and this policy will be updated before ads are switched on.

1b. Data Stored in Your Browser

To keep your work between visits, the calculator saves the following in your browser's local storage, on your device only. It is never transmitted to us:

  • Your calculator inputs and option legs.
  • Cached market data responses (to avoid repeat requests).
  • Your Alpha Vantage API key, once you enter one and use it. Emptying the API key field deletes it.

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it. If you use the "Copy shareable link" button, your inputs are encoded into the link itself, so anyone you send it to can see those inputs. Your API key is never part of a shared link or an exported file.

2. Use of Cookies

This site sets no cookies today. The calculator keeps what it needs in your browser's local storage instead, as described in section 1b.

  • Advertising (planned, not active yet): once ads are switched on, Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this or other websites.
  • Analytics: we do not use any. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

Your browser's settings let you block or delete cookies and site data at any time. For Google's advertising, see the links in section 5.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense – planned, to serve ads. Not active yet: nothing is loaded from Google on this site today.
    Learn more: How Google uses information from sites that use our services
  • jsDelivr – content delivery network used to load the charting library. Your IP address is visible to it, as with any web request.
  • U.S. Department of the Treasury (home.treasury.gov) – requested by your browser only when you ask for the risk-free rate, to read the published daily yield curve. No information about you is sent beyond the request itself.
  • Our own market data function (/api/market, hosted on Vercel) – when you ask for a futures price, volatility or convenience yield, your browser calls this site, and this site then reads public futures quotes from Yahoo Finance. Your browser never contacts Yahoo, and we send them nothing about you. The request is logged by our host (Vercel) the same way any page request is, and responses are cached so repeated requests do not leave this site.
  • Alpha Vantage (alphavantage.co) – an optional fallback, requested by your browser only if you supplied your own API key and the function above is unavailable. The key and the ticker you chose are sent directly from your browser to them, never through us.

These market-data requests are optional: the calculator works without them.

4. Data Security

We do not collect or store any personal data ourselves. All third-party data processing is governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to or deletion of your data held by third parties.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising.

Visit YourAdChoices or Google Ads Settings to manage your ad preferences.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy, about how the calculator handles data, or about the tool itself, the best route is to open an issue on the project's public repository:

github.com/MartinColaF/commodity-options-calculator/issues

Issues are public and need a GitHub account. If your question is a privacy request you would rather not make in public — asking what is held about you, or asking for it to be deleted — use the private address below instead. It is revealed on request rather than written into the page, to keep it away from address harvesters.
