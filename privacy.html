Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

Thank you for visiting Commodity Options Calculator. This website provides an educational tool for simulating options pricing for agricultural commodities.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information from users, and we run no server of our own: the calculator runs entirely in your browser. However, we may use third-party services like Google AdSense that may collect anonymized data through cookies or tracking technologies to display relevant ads.

1b. Data Stored in Your Browser

To keep your work between visits, the calculator saves the following in your browser's local storage, on your device only. It is never transmitted to us:

  • Your calculator inputs and option legs.
  • Cached market data responses (to avoid repeat requests).
  • Your Alpha Vantage API key, if you choose to enter one.

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it. If you use the "Copy shareable link" button, your inputs are encoded into the link itself, so anyone you send it to can see those inputs.

2. Use of Cookies

We use cookies for:

  • Advertising: Google AdSense may use cookies to serve personalized ads based on your previous visits to this or other websites.
  • Analytics (in the future): To understand how users interact with the site and improve the user experience.

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time through the cookie banner.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense – to serve ads.
    Learn more: How Google uses information from sites that use our services
  • jsDelivr – content delivery network used to load the charting library. Your IP address is visible to it, as with any web request.
  • U.S. Department of the Treasury (home.treasury.gov) – requested by your browser only when you ask for the risk-free rate, to read the published daily yield curve. No information about you is sent beyond the request itself.
  • Our own market data function (/api/market, hosted on Vercel) – when you ask for a futures price, volatility or convenience yield, your browser calls this site, and this site then reads public futures quotes from Yahoo Finance. Your browser never contacts Yahoo, and we send them nothing about you. The request is logged by our host (Vercel) the same way any page request is, and responses are cached so repeated requests do not leave this site.
  • Alpha Vantage (alphavantage.co) – an optional fallback, requested by your browser only if you supplied your own API key and the function above is unavailable. The key and the ticker you chose are sent directly from your browser to them, never through us.

These market-data requests are optional: the calculator works without them.

4. Data Security

We do not collect or store any personal data ourselves. All third-party data processing is governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to or deletion of your data held by third parties.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising.

Visit YourAdChoices or Google Ads Settings to manage your ad preferences.

github.com/MartinColaF/commodity-options-calculator/issues
