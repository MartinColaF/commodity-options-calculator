Black-76 is the market standard for options on commodity futures. Use Black-Scholes only when your underlying is the physical spot price.

Market data

Fills the futures price, risk-free rate, volatility and convenience yield for the selected commodity in one go. Each field reports its own source below, and anything that fails is left untouched.

Futures prices come from the front-month contract; the convenience yield is implied by two listed contracts on the real curve. The rate is the US Treasury daily par yield curve, interpolated to your expiry. When the two contracts sit close together, a small price gap annualises into a large yield — the two prices used are shown below so you can judge it.

Volatility settings

Historical (realised) volatility, not implied. Measured on the front-month futures itself, so roll gaps can inflate it slightly.

Carry, checked by hand

Inverts F = S·e(r−y)T, net of storage costs. The Yield button above fills these boxes with the two contracts it used, so you can check its work or override it.

Leave Vol blank to use the global volatility, or set it per leg to model a skew. Days can differ per leg for calendar spreads. Under is that leg's own delivery month price — blank uses the contract on the left, and the Leg prices button below fills it from the real curve. Entry overrides the theoretical premium with the price you actually paid; press IV to back out its implied volatility.

In a real commodity calendar each month trades at its own price. This reads the listed curve, measures its slope, and prices every leg at its own expiry. The payoff chart then moves all months together, in proportion.

Not investment advice. Prices come from a theoretical model, and the market-data helpers give historical or reference values, not live quotes or implied volatility. Verify against your broker before trading. American prices use the Barone-Adesi-Whaley approximation: typically within 0.01% of the underlying against a binomial tree, and within 0.6% at its worst, on two-year high-volatility contracts.
